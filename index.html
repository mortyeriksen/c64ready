      Setup C64 READY.

      C64 READY. needs Commodore's ROMs. They are copyrighted, so bring your own. Add them once and this browser remembers them.

      Get ROM files from VICE

      Have VICE installed? Pick its folder and the C64 ROMs are found inside. Your browser may call that an upload, but nothing is sent anywhere.

      VICE FOLDER
      Pick the top folder. Subfolders are searched too.
